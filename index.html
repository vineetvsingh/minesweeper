    No guessing

    A solver checks every board using only the numbers, so logic always finds the next safe square.

    Dig

    Click or tap a square. In Flag mode, a tap places a flag instead.

    Flag

    Right-click, long-press, or press F on a focused square.

    Clear around a number

    Click a number once all its mines are flagged to dig every other square next to it.

    Hint

    Rings the numbers that prove a square is safe and explains the step. Hint-free wins count for best times.

    Logic

    Gentle boards fall to one number at a time. Tough boards need several steps that compare two numbers.

    Daily & Share

    Daily gives everyone the same board each day. Share gives you a code that rebuilds any board.

    Keyboard

    Arrows move, Space digs, F flags, N new board, H hint, P pause, R retry, M dig/flag, + − zoom.

    Phones

    Pinch the board to zoom. Long-press to flag, or switch the tap action to Flag.
